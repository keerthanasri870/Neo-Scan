Hackathon prototype, not a medical device. This runs a simplified color-correction heuristic for demonstration purposes only — it is not clinically validated and must never be used to make real decisions about a newborn's health. Any real concern about jaundice needs an in-person clinical or lab assessment.

What's actually happening under the hood

The calibration patch gives the app a known neutral reference in this exact lighting. The skin patch color is corrected against that reference (a per-channel grey-world correction), then converted to CIE Lab color space, where the b* axis measures how far a color sits toward yellow. A higher corrected yellow value maps to a higher estimated bilirubin level, and that estimate is compared against age-appropriate thresholds to produce a Low / Watch / Refer Now flag — the same three-tier logic described in the pitch deck.
